A letter for Ria

Dear Ria,

I wanted to write you a note before your first day at college tomorrow. Starting at a new place can mean remembering a lot of small things, so I thought I would put some of them here. It might be useful to check your timetable tonight and make sure you know what time you need to leave in the morning.

You should check the route once more before you go, just in case there are any changes. Some buildings can look similar when you have not been there before, so keeping the address or a map on your phone could help. If you have to find a classroom, look at the room number and ask someone if you are not sure where it is.

Things to remember

  • college ID and any forms or papers
  • phone, charger, and headphones
  • timetable and the college address
  • water bottle and something to eat
  • keys, wallet, and anything else you normally carry

Try to get there a little early if you can. That gives you time to find the entrance and work out where you are going. If you do not know where something is, asking someone nearby is usually easier than walking around and guessing. Other people will probably be new too, and they may also be trying to find the right room.

There might be a lot of information during the day about classes, attendance, schedules, and different places on campus. You do not have to remember every single detail immediately. If someone gives you an important date or a room number, you can write it down in your phone or notebook and check it later.

It is also a good idea to eat something before you leave, because busy mornings can make it easy to forget. Charge your phone tonight, set an alarm, and put the things you need near your bag. That should make the morning a bit easier and mean you do not have to look for everything at the last minute.

If the first day feels confusing at any point, just take it one thing at a time. You can ask where to go, check the timetable again, or take a minute to figure out what is next. Most people need a little time to get used to a new place, so it is normal if everything does not make sense straight away.

When you get home, you can check if there is anything you need for the next day and charge your phone again. Try to get some rest too, because a full day can be tiring even if it goes well. I hope you have a good first day and that everything goes smoothly. Let me know how it went when you get a chance.

Take care,
Aaron

My Ria,

I’m so sorry for the ways I’ve behaved and for the ways I’ve treated you. I’ve been thinking about the hurt I caused, and I wish I could take those moments back. I never wanted to hurt you—not for a second. But I know that what I meant doesn’t erase how it felt for you, and I’m truly sorry.

I love you so much, and I still love you with the same heart I had at the beginning. That hasn’t changed through the arguments, the misunderstandings, or the distance between us. I’m not writing this to pressure you or ask you to forget what happened. I just want you to know that you matter to me more than I’ve been able to show.

I miss you—so much. I miss talking to you, laughing with you, sharing the little details of my day, and feeling close to you. I miss the comfort of knowing you’re there. I miss you, Ria. Some moments feel especially quiet without you in them.

I want to feel loved by you and cared for by you, and I want to give you that same love and care in return. I want us to be gentle with each other, to listen better, and to find our way through misunderstandings instead of letting them pull us apart. I know love isn’t just saying the right words. It’s showing up, taking responsibility, and doing better—and I want to keep learning how to do that.

These past few days have already been shit because of college and stuff, and on top of that, not having you. I’ve had exam pressure and so many assignments, and I’ve barely been sleeping—going to bed around 3 and waking up at 7. I’m not saying that to excuse how I treated you. You deserved kindness from me regardless. I just wanted to be honest about what’s been going on in my head and why I’ve felt so worn down.

I hope you’ve found good friends and kind roommates, and that college is giving you happy moments and people who make you feel at home. I imagine you’re busy with classes and friends, and I didn’t want to disturb you or make you feel like you had to answer me. I hope you’re doing well. I’ve just been missing you from over here, quietly wishing I could tell you about everything.

I made this website for you because I wanted to give my love somewhere to go—to make something that could remind you how special you are to me. Even with our fights and misunderstandings, you’re still the person I love. I hope you can feel the care behind it, and the truth behind these words.

I love you, Ria. Madly, deeply, and with all the tenderness I should have shown you more often. I’m sorry for the hurt I caused. I miss you every day, and I hope that, when you’re ready, we can talk and understand each other better.

Whatever happens next, I want you to know this: the love I have for you is real, and it has been there from the beginning. You are so precious to me. You’re always my cute little baby...

Always,
Yours ❤️❤️❤️
